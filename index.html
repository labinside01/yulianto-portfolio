About me

A researcher's rigor, an engineer's craft, a creator's eye.

I'm Yulianto, a researcher and multidisciplinary engineer based in Indonesia. My work spans electronic engineering and service, IoT systems, artificial intelligence, full-stack web and Flutter mobile development, alongside photography, videography, YouTube and content creation.

I enjoy turning evidence into systems that work in the real world — from connected devices and intelligent inspection tools to research portals and visual stories.
